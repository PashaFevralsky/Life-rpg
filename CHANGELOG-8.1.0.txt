Life RPG 8.1.0 — Modern Visual System
=====================================

- visual-only redesign on top of 8.0.4 business logic;
- quieter dark palette with one primary accent;
- removed gradient-heavy/button-heavy dashboard feel;
- flatter cards and nested surfaces;
- modern underline sub-tabs instead of large pills;
- redesigned bottom navigation with restrained active state;
- mobile global profile header now scrolls away; section navigation stays sticky;
- fixed translucent sticky-header content bleed;
- contextual FAB hides on Settings, Forecast and Today/Progress;
- improved safe-area spacing;
- modern bottom-sheet modals;
- stronger form focus states and larger mobile controls;
- simplified progress bars, tags, KPI tiles and quick actions;
- reading queue/import remains intact;
- state schema stays v16.
