Life RPG 10.2.0 — Direction & Execution
Base: Life RPG 10.1.0, state schema v17

NEW — Goals / Horizons OS
- 90-day, annual and custom-horizon goals.
- Outcome, priority, deadline, area and progress.
- Link one goal to multiple existing projects.
- When projects are linked, goal progress is calculated automatically from project progress.
- Deadline / behind-pace detection.
- Goal deadlines appear in Calendar OS.
- At-risk goals can become Life OS actions.
- Goal completion awards result XP once.

NEW — Habits / Routines OS
- Repeating routines by weekday.
- Area, priority and estimated minutes.
- One-tap completion from Today.
- Weekly and 28-day adherence facts.
- Missed past routines do NOT become overdue tasks.
- Routines only exist statistically after their creation date.
- Future routine commitments appear in Calendar OS and capacity calculations.
- Completed routine counts as an active day and awards process XP once per completion.

NEW — Search / Command Palette
- Global mobile button in the header.
- Ctrl/Cmd + K on desktop.
- Search Tasks, Projects, Goals, Routines, Inbox, CRM, books and manual Calendar events.
- Quick commands for new Task, Inbox capture, Goal, Project, Routine, Calendar and Review.
- No new persistent state.

INSIGHTS OS 2
- Goal portfolio snapshot.
- Task flow over 28 days.
- Routine adherence over 28 days.
- Routine adherence ↔ Life Score correlation only after 4 saved weekly snapshots.
- Existing correlations keep minimum-sample requirements and explicitly remain non-causal.

CROSS-DOMAIN INTEGRATION
- Goals -> Life OS + Calendar OS + Review snapshots.
- Routines -> Life OS + Calendar capacity + Review snapshots + Gamification active day.
- Review plan stores up to 2 focus goals in addition to projects.
- Rules OS warns if more than 5 goals are simultaneously active.
- Calendar summary now exposes goal and routine events.
