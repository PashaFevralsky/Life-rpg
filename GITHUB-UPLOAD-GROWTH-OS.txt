Life RPG — Growth OS feature pack
Compatible base: 11.1.1 / STATE_VERSION 18

WHAT THIS ADDS
1) Universal Tracking Engine
   - single timeline from reading, tennis, routines, completed tasks and custom trackers
   - tracker types: tick, flexible habit (N/week), number, 1–10 range, timer
   - plan vs fact for today's minutes
   - 35-day activity heatmap

2) Habit Strength
   - weighted 90-day strength instead of fragile all-or-nothing streaks
   - 30-day adherence and trend
   - flexible habits with weekly targets

3) Knowledge OS 2
   - reading speed and finish forecast
   - ideas / questions / quotes / applications
   - tags and lightweight topic graph
   - spaced review queue (1 / 7 / 30 / 90 / 180 days)
   - reading/knowledge heatmap

4) Tennis OS 2
   - reusable training templates
   - exact drill-minute split for saved sessions
   - technique exposure dashboard
   - acute vs baseline load
   - match results vs stronger / peer / lower-rated opponents

5) RPG Growth 2
   - XP-backed domain levels
   - evidence dashboard
   - milestones based on actual logged behavior
   - no penalties for missed days
